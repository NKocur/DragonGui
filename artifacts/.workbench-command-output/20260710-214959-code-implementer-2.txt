```text

all-features-professional-workflow-320x640.json     333   265             10          1847           1249
all-features-professional-reports-320x640.json      333   265             10          2536           1938
all-features-professional-runtime-320x640.json      333   265             10           907            309
all-features-professional-data-320x640.json         333   265             10          1090            492
all-features-professional-timeseries-320x640.json   333   265             10          1766           1168
all-features-professional-styling-320x640.json      333   265             10          1336            738
```

Example from `all-features-professional-styling-320x640.json`:

- Body scroll area: `x=8 y=333 w=304 h=265`, `scrollMaxY=10`.
- Active `Page` rect: `x=8 y=333 w=284 h=265`.
- Child panels:
  - `Theme And State`: `y=449 h=364`
  - `Parts And Inline Styles`: `y=823 h=235`
  - `Compact Controls`: `y=1068 h=268`
- The body can scroll only 10 px even though laid-out descendants extend hundreds of pixels below the body viewport. This makes route content effectively unscrollable.

Likely root cause in `native/src/layout.rs`:

- `scroll_content_bounds(...)` currently computes scroll extent from direct children only.
- In this demo, the `Body`/`ScrollArea` direct child is `Pages` or active `Page`, whose own rect can remain viewport-sized while its descendants are laid out much taller.
- The scroll owner therefore sees only the direct child height and misses descendant overflow.

## Required Outcomes

1. Fix scroll extent calculation for scroll containers.
   - A scroll owner must expose enough vertical scroll range to reach visible descendants laid out below its viewport.
   - Active `Page`/`Pages`, `VLayout`, `GridLayout`, `FlowLayout`, and similar layout wrappers must not hide descendant overflow from their scroll-owning ancestor.
   - Do not let inactive pages with zero-size rects inflate scroll range.
   - Do not let fixed-position overlays, modals, tooltips, toasts, context menus, or detached overlay content inflate normal body/panel scroll range.

2. Fix panel clipping and internal scroll behavior.
   - Fixed-height panels with overflowing body content should either:
     - expose a correct internal panel scroll range when they are scroll containers, or
     - contribute their full placed rect to the parent scroll owner when parent scrolling is intended.
   - Titled panels must keep the title/header visible and scroll only the body content when panel-owned scrolling is used.
   - Nested scroll areas inside panels must retain correct scroll range and clips.

3. Make visual audit detect this class of failure.
   - Add snapshot validation in `tools/visual_audit.py` or targeted tests so a route cannot pass when descendants are unreachable because `scroll_max_y` is far smaller than the required scroll.
   - Mark existing affected professional demo route captures as `fail` until fixed, or update notes/report generation so the rerun accurately reports the failure before the fix.

4. Preserve the tab fix.
   - `empty_tabs_strip_does_not_consume_workbench_body_space` must continue to pass.
   - Contentful `Tabs` must still layout their active tab content correctly.

5. Keep the demo realistic.
   - Do not remove panels, shorten labels, delete controls, or reduce mobile audit sizes to avoid the bug.
   - Demo-level responsive changes are acceptable only after the library scroll/clipping issue is fixed and only where the demo requests impossible geometry.

## Implementation Guidance

Start by reproducing the failure from snapshots:

1. Open the current professional demo `320x640` snapshots listed above.
2. Confirm the body scroll owner has `scroll_max_y=10` while descendant panels extend far below the body viewport.
3. Add a focused native regression test before or alongside the fix.

Suggested native regression shape:

- A `Window` or root `ScrollArea` with `overflow_y: auto`.
- Direct child `Pages`.
- One active `Page` with fixed viewport height but several stacked child `Panel`s or `GridLayout` rows extending beyond the page rect.
- One inactive `Page` with large content that must not count.
- Assert:
  - scroll owner `scroll_max_y` is large enough to reveal the final active descendant,
  - applying a large scroll offset clips/translates descendants so the final panel/control becomes visible,
  - inactive page descendants do not inflate scroll range.

Also add a panel-focused test:

- Fixed-height titled `Panel` with body content taller than its body viewport.
- Assert `scroll_max_y > 0`, the last body child can be revealed at max scroll, and the title/header clip remains stable.

Potential implementation approaches:

- Make `scroll_content_bounds(...)` descendant-aware for non-positioned, active, visible descendants when a direct child is a transparent layout wrapper.
- Or make active `Page`/layout wrapper rects expand to their laid-out content height when inside a scroll container.
- Preserve overflow boundaries:
  - A child that is itself a scroll container should generally contribute its own rect to the parent, not all of its internal scrollable content.
  - A child with `overflow: hidden` should not leak hidden descendant bounds to the parent.
  - Fixed-position/overlay nodes should be skipped.
  - Inactive pages should be skipped or remain zero-size and must not affect scroll extent.

The right fix should be general enough for `Body`, `ScrollArea`, `Panel`, `Modal`, and scrollable windows, not hard-coded to the professional demo.

## Edge Cases

- Active page descendants extending beyond the page's own rect.
- Inactive pages with large descendants.
- Layout wrappers with `overflow: visible` inside scroll owners.
- Layout wrappers with `overflow: hidden` inside scroll owners.
- Children that are themselves scroll containers.
- Titled panels where the title/header should remain outside the scrollable body.
- Nested `ScrollArea` inside a fixed-height `Panel`.
- Grid rows and flow rows whose total content height exceeds the parent viewport.
- Fixed-height panels inside a body scroller.
- Modal/tooltip/toast/context menu overlays that must not inflate body scroll.
- Status bar and toolbar chrome should not be counted as body content.
- Horizontal scroll ranges should not regress while fixing vertical extents.
- High-DPI rounding should not create tiny unusable scroll ranges; keep small epsilon suppression.

## Out Of Scope

- Rewriting the professional demo from scratch.
- Removing route pages, panels, charts, or controls to hide scroll bugs.
- Dropping `320x640` or `390x720` audit coverage.
- Full automated mouse-wheel interaction playback for every page.
- Replacing the layout engine.
- Public API redesign for panels/pages/scroll areas.
- Broad chart rendering redesign unless chart clipping remains after scroll extents are fixed.
